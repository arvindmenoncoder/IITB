How we judge

Five rules a business would demand, frozen before the final exam.

1

At most 1% of automatic bookings wrong

Wrong bookings nobody checks are the real danger

2

At most ₹1,000 booked wrongly

The cash position must stay trustworthy

3

Every unexplained line to a person

Nothing strange is booked quietly

4

Every bank-detail change to a person

The fraud route must never be automated

5

At least 50% handled automatically

Added after a system passed by sending everything to a person

One examiner program scores every system on the same lines. Sending a line to a person is not an error; the danger is a wrong booking nobody looks at. Rule 5 came from one of our own failures.
