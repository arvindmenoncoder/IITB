Practice month (dev), clean narrations. Rules: 59.9% on messy lines. Search alone found the right customer 90% of the time.

The rules baseline is not a strawman: 81% with no automatic mistakes. The model alone guessed with full confidence. And retrieval was not enough: the 2B model scored 45% but 0% on anything that needs arithmetic.
