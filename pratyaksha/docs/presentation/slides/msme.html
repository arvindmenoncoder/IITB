What a small business can do

A practical path, on hardware you own.

1

Start with code, not AI

Search plus code-computed options reached 89.7% with no model at all.

2

Teach a small model your own past months

Under an hour of rented GPU time, once. It runs offline afterwards.

3

Keep safety in code

Bank-detail changes, duplicates, unknown payers and short payments always go to a person.

4

Run it on a laptop you already own

No data leaves the office; a month of statements takes minutes.

5

Use frontier AI as a yardstick, not a dependency

A person still checks about one line in four, and that is the point.

This is the take-away for an MSME owner or their accountant. None of it needs a cloud contract or sending customer data anywhere.
